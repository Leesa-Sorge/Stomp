# 🥾 Western Pest Control — The Stomp

> **"Here For Good" — Since 2001 · St. George, UT**

A comical canvas animation where a Western Pest Control pest professional's boot stomps 5 DIY pest control imposters — each one a lovingly drawn insect wearing the logo of a fake competitor.

---

## 🎬 Animation Overview

Five rounds. Five bugs. One boot.

| Round | Bug | Fake Company | Their Tagline |
|-------|-----|--------------|---------------|
| 1 | 🪳 Cockroach | **Your Husband's Pest Control** | *"He watched a YouTube video!"* |
| 2 | 🐜 Ant | **Throw Your Shoe At It Pest Control** | *"Now you're barefoot AND infested!"* |
| 3 | 🕷️ Spider | **Pinterest DIY Pest Solutions** | *"Lavender oil repels EVERYTHING!"* |
| 4 | 🦂 Scorpion | **My Neighbor Dave's Exterminating Co.** | *"He did his own house once!"* |
| 5 | 🐛 Silverfish | **Hope & Pray Pest Management** | *"Maybe they'll just leave?"* |

## 🚀 Deploying to GitHub Pages

1. **Fork or clone** this repository
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**
3. Set source to **"Deploy from a branch"** → `main` → `/ (root)`
4. Click **Save**
5. Your site will be live at: `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/western-stomp/`

## 🐛 The Bugs

Each insect is **fully procedurally drawn** with:
- Animated legs / appendages (walk cycle)
- Body segments with gradients & lighting
- Eyes, antennae, and species-specific features
- A **company logo badge** on their back
- **Squish deformation** on impact
- **Entrance slide-in** animation per round
